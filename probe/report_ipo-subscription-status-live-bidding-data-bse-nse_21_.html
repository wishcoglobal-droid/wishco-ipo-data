Live Mainboard IPOs Subscription Status from BSE and NSE: 2026

This report provides real-time demand of IPOs during the bidding period from BSE and NSE, reflecting how many times an IPO has been subscribed by investors compared to the shares offered.

The Live IPO Subscription Status on BSE and NSE shows real-time demand for the issue during the bidding period. It reflects subscription levels across investor categories, including QIBs, NIIs, and Retail, based on combined bidding data from both exchanges. This helps investors assess the level of interest in the IPO, enabling them to take better-informed investment decisions.

Investors can submit their IPO applications through either exchange (BSE or NSE), and the consolidated data reflects the overall market interest in the issue.

    • Closing Date: The last day on which investors can apply for shares in that IPO. After this date, no further bids or applications are accepted.
